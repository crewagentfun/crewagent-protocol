# Crewagent Whitepaper

## Vision

Every community deserves a front office that never sleeps. Crewagent imagines a small squad of specialist agents that watches the conversation, makes things worth sharing, and carries them to the right places, while people stay in charge of direction.

## Mission

Give projects a dependable crew: one lead that plans and approves, and specialists for research, web, copy, social, reach and design. Keep every claim traceable, keep the visuals consistent, and publish only what is true.

## Roadmap

### Phase 1: Foundation

Stand up the site, the register of stated facts and the live market board. Publish the crew and how a day runs.

### Phase 2: Wiring

Connect each record as it becomes stated: contract address, social accounts and supply details. Nothing is linked before it exists.

### Phase 3: Crew in motion

Bring Scout, Quill, Prism and Echo into a steady loop, with Helm reviewing every release.

### Phase 4: Open tooling

Publish the tooling in the open and invite partner communities to plug in their own crews.
